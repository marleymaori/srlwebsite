SRL Rules & Playing Protocols

Official touch rugby guidelines, tackle limits, and post-match official reviews.

Core Game Rules

  • 1. No Tackling: Full contact or tackling is strictly prohibited. Possession changes are governed by a 2-hand touch rule on the ball carrier.
  • 2. 6 Tackles Limit: Each team is allocated a maximum of 6 touches (tackles) to advance the ball before turnover on the 6th touch.

Referee Protocol & Reviews

  • 1. Whistle Protocol: Match stages (half-time, full-time) are officially concluded upon the official whistle.
  • 2. Post-Match Assessment: Referees review games after completion for fair play, foul conduct, and try verification.
