SRL Touch Rugby Rules & Protocols

Official regulations, gameplay limitations, and referee post-match review standards.

Core Gameplay Rules

  • No Tackling: Full contact or tackling is strictly prohibited. Possession changes are governed by a 2-hand touch rule on the ball carrier.
  • 6 Tackles Only: Each team is allocated a maximum of 6 touches (tackles) to advance the ball and score before a turnover on the 6th touch.

Referee Review & Whistle Protocol

  • Final Whistle Stages: Once the final whistle blows, the game is officially marked as either half-time or full-time depending on the match stage.
  • Post-Match Review: After full-time, referees review the entire game to check for rule violations, fair play, and invalid tries.
